Why we exist

Many families in Nigeria raise a child or care for a relative with an intellectual or developmental disability with little information, little support and a great deal of stigma.

Whole Person Ability Initiative exists to change that. We help families understand what they are facing, find the right health and education services, and be welcomed by their communities. Every person has abilities, and our job is to help those abilities be seen.

What we do

  • Awareness

    Talks and campaigns in schools, churches and communities that replace myths about disability with facts.

  • Family support

    Meetups and guidance for parents and caregivers, so no family has to face this alone.

  • Referrals

    Connecting families to health professionals, therapists and schools that can support their child.

  • Early signs

    Helping parents and teachers spot developmental delays early, when support can do the most good.

  • Inclusion

    Speaking up for the rights and dignity of people with disabilities, and building skills and livelihood opportunities.

Need urgent help?

We are a new initiative and do not provide medical treatment or emergency services. In an emergency, contact emergency services or go to your nearest hospital. For ongoing care, speak to a doctor or qualified health professional.
