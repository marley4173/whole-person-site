Who we are

Whole Person Ability Initiative is an organisation established to promote the dignity, inclusion, empowerment and equal opportunities of children and individuals living with disabilities.

We believe that every person has abilities, potential and value, regardless of their physical, intellectual, developmental or other disabilities.

Our organisation seeks to create a more understanding and inclusive society by educating communities, supporting families, encouraging inclusive education and advocating for equal opportunities.

We recognise that meaningful inclusion begins with awareness, acceptance and action. Through community engagement, educational initiatives, advocacy and practical support, we aim to make a positive difference in the lives of individuals and families affected by disabilities.

Although our journey begins within local communities, our vision is global. We aspire to build partnerships and initiatives that contribute to a world where everyone has the opportunity to participate, grow and thrive.

Our belief: There Is Ability in Every Disability.

Our vision and mission

Our Vision

To build a world where children and individuals living with disabilities are valued, accepted, included and empowered to live fulfilling lives with equal opportunities, dignity and respect.

Our Mission

To support children and individuals living with disabilities, promote inclusion and equal opportunities, and educate communities, families and institutions on understanding, accepting and supporting people with disabilities.

Through awareness, advocacy, education and community engagement, we strive to create environments where everyone has the opportunity to learn, participate, grow and thrive.

Our strategic objectives

Through our work, we aim to:

  1. Promote Inclusion

    Support the well-being, development and meaningful inclusion of children and individuals living with disabilities.

  2. Raise Awareness

    Increase public understanding of different disabilities and challenge stigma, discrimination and misconceptions.

  3. Educate and Empower Families

    Provide families, caregivers and communities with information and guidance on how to understand and support people with disabilities.

  4. Encourage Inclusive Education

    Promote equal access to education, learning resources and opportunities for children with disabilities.

  5. Facilitate Access to Support

    Help connect families with educational materials, assistive devices, relevant professionals and essential support services.

  6. Promote Rights and Dignity

    Advocate for the dignity, rights, participation and equal opportunities of people living with disabilities.

  7. Build Strategic Partnerships

    Collaborate with schools, healthcare professionals, nonprofits, businesses and other institutions to advance our mission.

  8. Develop Sustainable Initiatives

    Establish meaningful community-based initiatives with the potential to grow and make a wider impact internationally.

What we do

Our work is guided by the belief that every individual deserves the opportunity to live with dignity, participate in society and reach their full potential.

  1. Disability Awareness and Community Education

    We aim to educate communities about disabilities, challenge misconceptions, reduce stigma and encourage understanding, acceptance and inclusion.

    Through awareness campaigns, educational visits, community discussions and outreach activities, we hope to help people understand how they can support individuals living with disabilities.

  2. Child Support and Empowerment

    We are committed to promoting the well-being, development, education and inclusion of children living with disabilities.

    Our proposed initiatives will focus on creating opportunities for children to learn, participate, develop their abilities and experience an environment where they feel valued and supported.

  3. Family and Caregiver Support

    Families and caregivers play an important role in supporting children and individuals living with disabilities.

    We aim to provide educational guidance, relevant resources, awareness and practical assistance to families, while helping connect them with appropriate professional and community support services.

  4. Inclusive Education and School Outreach

    We seek to encourage inclusive learning environments where children with disabilities are respected, supported and given opportunities to participate in education.

    Our planned school outreach activities will promote disability awareness among students, teachers and school communities.

  5. Advocacy and Equal Opportunities

    We advocate for the dignity, rights, participation and equal opportunities of people living with disabilities.

    We aim to encourage institutions, organisations and communities to recognise individual abilities and create environments that promote accessibility, acceptance and inclusion.

Supporting children and families

We understand that some families face financial and practical challenges when seeking appropriate support for children living with disabilities.

Whole Person Ability Initiative aims to provide needs-based assistance that responds to the actual needs of children and their families.

Rather than providing unrestricted cash assistance, our approach will focus on practical support such as:

  • Educational materials, school supplies and learning resources.
  • Assistive devices and mobility support where appropriate.
  • Assistance connecting families with qualified healthcare and therapy professionals.
  • Essential supplies and disability-related resources.
  • Guidance and educational resources for parents and caregivers.
  • Referrals to relevant support organisations and service providers.

Our goal is to help reduce barriers that prevent children living with disabilities from accessing essential resources, education and opportunities.

All assistance will be guided by available resources, individual needs, appropriate professional advice and responsible accountability.

His belief: There Is Ability in Every Disability.

Understanding the conditions

  • Intellectual disability

    Significant differences in learning, reasoning and everyday skills that begin in childhood. With the right support, people with an intellectual disability learn and grow.

  • Down syndrome

    A genetic condition caused by an extra copy of chromosome 21. It happens by chance and is not caused by anything a parent did.

  • Autism

    A difference in how a person communicates, interacts and experiences the world. It is a spectrum, so every autistic person is different.

  • Developmental delay

    When a child reaches milestones such as walking or talking later than expected. Spotting it early means support can start sooner.

How you can support someone

  • Speak to the person, not about them.
  • Be patient and give them time.
  • Celebrate small steps.
  • Talk to a health professional early if you notice delays.
  • Include them in school, church and community life.

Privacy notice

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Whole Person Ability Initiative, based in Abuja, Nigeria. You can reach us at wholepersonability@gmail.com.

What we collect

Only what each form asks for: your name, your contact details and the information you choose to type. We do not ask for children’s names, photos, medical records or ID numbers, and we ask you not to include them.

Why we collect it

To reply to your request, and to match volunteers, partners and families with the right next step. We do not sell your details or use them for marketing.

Who sees it

Your form is sent by email to our official inbox. An email delivery service and our website host process it for us so that it can arrive. We do not keep a separate database of submissions on the website.

How long we keep it

Only for as long as we need it to respond and to keep sensible records. After that we delete it.

Requests involving children

Family support requests must be made by a parent or caregiver. We collect as little as possible about the child: the type of support needed and whatever you choose to describe.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information you sent us. Email us and we will help. We aim to handle personal information responsibly and in line with Nigerian data protection law.
