Privacy notice

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Whole Person Ability Initiative, based in Abuja, Nigeria. You can reach us at wholepersonability@gmail.com.

What we collect

Only what each form asks for: your name, your contact details and the information you choose to type. We do not ask for children’s names, photos, medical records or ID numbers, and we ask you not to include them.

Why we collect it

To reply to your request, and to match volunteers, partners and families with the right next step. We do not sell your details or use them for marketing.

Who sees it

Your form is sent by email to our official inbox. An email delivery service and our website host process it for us so that it can arrive. We do not keep a separate database of submissions on the website.

How long we keep it

Only for as long as we need it to respond and to keep sensible records. After that we delete it.

Requests involving children

Family support requests must be made by a parent or caregiver. We collect as little as possible about the child: the type of support needed and whatever you choose to describe.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information you sent us. Email us and we will help. We aim to handle personal information responsibly and in line with Nigerian data protection law.
